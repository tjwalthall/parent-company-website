Outside Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Outside Timer is published by Parent Company LLC. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information stored on the device

App preferences, settings, sessions, routines, progress, or other app information may be stored locally on your device unless a future version explicitly introduces another storage or synchronization method.

Information we collect

Parent Company LLC does not currently operate user accounts for Outside Timer, sell personal information, or use third-party advertising within the app.

Apple services

Outside Timer may use Apple-provided technologies or services where appropriate, including iCloud, notifications, Live Activities, App Store purchasing and analytics, or crash diagnostics made available by Apple. The exact services used may differ by app version. Apple processes information according to its own privacy policies.

Permissions

Outside Timer may request optional system permissions needed for features such as notifications or Live Activities. Locations, when entered, are intended to be entered manually rather than through location permission. You can review and manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Children’s privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parent Company LLC does not knowingly operate account-based services that collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version will remain available on this page, with the revision date shown above.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to hello@parentcompanyllc.com.
