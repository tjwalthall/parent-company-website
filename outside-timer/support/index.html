Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Outside Timer does not require an account.

How do I correct a session?

Open History, tap the session, then edit its date, duration, family members, location, or note.

Can I add outdoor time manually?

Yes. Use History to add a session for outdoor time that wasn’t recorded with the timer.

What if I forget a running timer?

You can enable a running-timer reminder in Settings. On supported devices, you can also stop the timer from the Live Activity on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.

Can I change my yearly goal?

Yes. Your outdoor-hours goal can be changed at any time in the app.

Can I sync or export my data?

Not in version 1.0.

How do I delete data?

Delete individual sessions or family members in the app. Removing the app deletes locally stored app data, subject to any device backups you control.
