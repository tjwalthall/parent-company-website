Privacy Policy for Outside Timer

Effective: September 2026

Outside Timer is designed to keep your family’s outdoor-time information private and on your device.

Information You Enter

You may enter family-member names, outdoor session dates and durations, optional locations and notes, a yearly goal, and app preferences. Outside Timer stores this information locally on your device. Parent Company LLC does not receive it.

Data Collection and Tracking

Outside Timer does not create an account, serve advertising, use analytics, or track you across apps or websites. The app does not transmit personal data to Parent Company LLC or third parties.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you enable timer reminders, the app may request notification permission and schedule reminders on your device. Live Activities display an active timer on supported Apple devices. These features do not require a Parent Company LLC-operated server.

Device Backups and Deletion

Apple device-backup features may include app data according to your Apple settings. Parent Company LLC cannot access those backups. You can delete information in the app, or remove the app to delete locally stored data, subject to any device backup you control.

Children

Outside Timer is intended for parents, caregivers, and families. Parent Company LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Questions can be sent to hello@parentcompanyllc.com.
